Try it yourself
1
Stand in front of the camera
You'll appear with a red box — the system doesn't know you yet. The board detects and processes every face in frame independently.
2
Open LinkedIn → Share Profile → QR
Hold your phone's LinkedIn QR code up to the camera. The app reads it and starts a 10-second authorization — a violet burst will appear on-screen.
3
Hold still inside the ring
An "O" enrollment ring appears. Place your face inside it and slowly turn your head. The app captures ~30 frames over ~15 seconds for a diverse set of angles.
4
Step back — you're recognized
Your box turns green with your LinkedIn name and a similarity score. Try moving further away or obscuring your face — watch it recover.
LinkedIn QR
Open LinkedIn → tap your photo → Share Profile → QR Code
Any LinkedIn profile QR authorizes enrollment. The app reads it in frame — no NFC, no app to install. TTL: 2 minutes.
